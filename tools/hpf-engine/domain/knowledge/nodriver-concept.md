## Semantic Layer
Nodriver is a library that connects to the browser directly via CDP over WebSocket, bypassing WebDriver entirely.

## Narrative Layer
By connecting directly to CDP, nodriver avoids the overhead and limitations of Selenium/WebDriver. It is Python-native, async, and avoids the Selenium binary dependency.
