# YouTube Short — 30 Second Script

Title: "Why your scraper gets blocked instantly"

---

Your scraper got blocked in the first minute.

You think it's the headless browser.

It's not.

It's the automation signals you never hid.

Anti-bot scripts check the page the moment it loads:

navigator.webdriver.
plugins.
canvas.

If you patch them after the page loads, it's too late.

Fix it at document creation:

addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument.

The browser thinks it's a normal visitor — because all the signals say so.
