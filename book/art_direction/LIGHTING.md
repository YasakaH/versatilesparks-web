# Lighting Reference

## Setup

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Key light | 45° above subject, slightly camera-right |
| Fill light | Camera-left, 30% intensity of key |
| Edge/rim light | Behind subject, camera-left, cyan tint |
| Ambient | Dark environment, minimal fill |

## Light Types

### Key Light (Primary)

Softbox, 60x80cm, positioned 45° above and 30° to the right of camera. Creates the primary shape and volume of the subject.

- Intensity: 100%
- Color: Cool white (5500K)
- Falloff: Soft, gradual
- Shadow: To camera-left, 30-40% opacity

### Fill Light (Secondary)

Softbox, 40x60cm, positioned camera-left at subject height. Reduces contrast ratio and fills shadow areas.

- Intensity: 30% of key
- Color: Cool white (5500K)
- Falloff: Very soft
- Purpose: Prevent harsh shadows, reveal detail in dark areas

### Rim Light (Edge)

Strip light or tube, positioned behind subject, offset camera-left, pointing toward camera. Creates the thin cyan edge highlight that separates subject from background.

- Intensity: 40%
- Color: Electric Cyan (`#00D4FF`) at 20% opacity
- Width: 1-2px on the subject's edge
- Purpose: Define the subject's edge against dark background

### Ambient Glow

Large, low-intensity fill from the background. Creates atmospheric depth.

- Intensity: 10%
- Color: Electric Cyan or Azure Blue at 5-10% opacity
- Radius: 200-400px behind the main subject
- Purpose: Background atmosphere, depth

## Prohibited Lighting

- ❌ Harsh directional light with hard shadows
- ❌ Multi-directional lighting (more than 3 lights)
- ❌ Strong backlight that obscures subject details
- ❌ Lens flare effects
- ❌ Colored gels on key or fill (only rim light uses color)
- ❌ Shadows with opacity > 40%
- ❌ Light sources visible in frame
