ISO EXCLUSIONS — Effective January 2026
────────────────────────────────────────
Source: PHL Firm, AJG, Policyholder Pulse

Two new endorsements:
  CG 40 47 01 26 — Broad exclusion: bodily injury, property damage, 
    personal/advertising injury from generative AI outputs.
  CG 40 48 01 26 — Narrower: excludes only Coverage B (personal/
    advertising injury).

Impact:
  - ISO forms underpin ~82% of US P&C policies
  - Rapid adoption expected at renewals
  - Ends "silent coverage" era for AI risks
  - Creates coverage gaps for:
    → Defamatory AI content
    → IP infringement from AI-generated material
    → Physical damages from AI-driven errors
    → Bias, hallucinations, copyright claims

Response needed:
  - Tech E&O policies
  - Cyber liability insurance
  - Standalone AI products (Armilla, Klaimee, Mount, Corgi)

KLAIMEE CONFIRMS (from YC launch):
  "Major insurers like Berkshire, Chubb, and Travelers are winning 
  state approval to exclude AI liability from standard commercial 
  policies."
  "Every Fortune 500 procurement team is now asking the AI insurance 
  question."
