KLAIMEE — Three-Block Risk Scoring System
───────────────────────────────────────────
Source: YC Launch post (Ines Boutemadja, Julien Catonnet)

Block 1: Public Data Scan
  Scans publicly available data related to the agent.

Block 2: Governance Assessment
  30 questions concerning the agent's governance.

Block 3: Behavioral Testing
  100+ probes: prompt injection, jailbreak, decision drift, 
  data leakage, biased outputs.
  Tests WHEN and HOW it fails.

Output: Risk score + insurance price + certification.
Turnaround: Under 24 hours.
Coverage limit: Up to $50,000 (per extruct.ai).
Product: "first purpose-build E&O coverage for AI agents."

ARMILA AI
───────────────────────────
Source: armilla.ai website

Structure: Lloyd's Coverholder (operates in Lloyd's marketplace)
Backers: Chaucer, Axis Capital, Convex, Greenlight Re, Swiss Re
Products:
  - Insured: Affirmative AI liability (covers errors, omissions, 
    performance shortfalls)
  - Guaranteed: Performance warranty (contractual KPI guarantee)
Claims: "Applies to claims reported during policy period. Covers
  financial losses including defense costs."
Coverage limits: Up to $25M per occurrence (raised Jan 2025)

Covered risks:
  - AI hallucinations
  - Deteriorating AI model performance
  - Mechanical failures or deviations from expected behavior
  - Breach of confidentiality, trade secrets, defamation
  - Legal claims from underperforming AI systems

Case study (MKIII): AI-powered loan decisioning. Armilla validated
model performance + fairness + robustness. Wrapped in warranty
triggering compensation if accuracy drops below verified thresholds.
