EXISTING PLAYERS (Evidence/Attestation Layer)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ProofSnap (getproofsnap.com)
   Product: "AI Flight Recorder"
   Method: Blockchain timestamp (SHA-256 + OpenTimestamps + Bitcoin)
   Focus: Chatbot outputs, individual AI responses
   Target: EU AI Act compliance, chatbot liability defense
   Limitation: Captures individual outputs, NOT full agent execution
     chains (tool calls, decisions, state changes)

2. TrueScreen (truescreen.io)
   Product: "Data Authenticity Platform"
   Method: QTSP digital seal + qualified timestamp (eIDAS Regulation)
   Focus: Digital content certification with legal value
   Target: European enterprises, regulatory compliance
   Limitation: General data certification, not agent-specific

3. SWEAR (security industry)
   Product: Digital evidence authentication
   Focus: Video/footage authenticity
   Target: Physical security industry

THE GAP THEY DON'T COVER
─────────────────────────
All existing players focus on OUTPUT attestation:
  "Here is proof that this specific output existed at this time."

None focus on EXECUTION provenance:
  "Here is the complete trace of tool calls, decisions, state changes,
   and evidence that led to this output."

Our EPR format captures:
  - Agent identity (who acted)
  - Tool calls (what was done)
  - State changes (what changed)
  - Evidence artifacts (screenshots, API responses)
  - Duration (how long)
  - Errors (what failed)
  - Sequence (order of operations)

THE TTL/EXPIRY PRIMITIVE
─────────────────────────
Uniquely identified by Björn Roberg blog (Apr 2026). No shipping product
has this. The insight: risk changes as agents drift. A static attestation
is worthless after the environment changes. Need: "Record → Replay → Expire"
with TTL-bound certification that re-verifies on drift.
