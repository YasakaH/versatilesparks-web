Date: 2026-07-19 11:20:45

  What they do: Log aggregation & SIEM
  Their focus: Post-hoc log analysis, not real-time verification
  Threat to us: LOW — reactive, not proactive

WHY THEY WON'T BUILD VERIFIABLE EXECUTION:
----------------------------------------
  Splunk is a log search engine. It's reactive (look up what happened). Verification is proactive (confirm it happened correctly). Splunk's query model can't express 'did this action have the intended effect?'
