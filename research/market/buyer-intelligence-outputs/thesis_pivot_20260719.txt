# DECISIONS

## 1. NAME CHANGE
Old: Automation Reliability / Verifiable Execution
New: Execution Proof

Rationale: "Proof" is concrete and customer-facing. "Verifiable Execution" is 
a category name that customers don't look for. "Execution Proof" says what 
you deliver.

Key insight: Rank by PROBABILITY OF STARTUP SUCCESS, not severity of pain.

## 3. PRODUCT SCOPE
Old: Verify everything (browsers, APIs, AI agents, RPA, procurement, healthcare, logistics)
New: Verify ONE thing first: Every AI tool call → Verified.

MVP Definition:
  "Every AI tool call → Verified."
  Nothing else. That's the product.

## 4. COMPETITIVE NARRATIVE
Old: "Incumbents won't build this because they profit from ambiguity."
Problem: This is a weak argument. Datadog monetizes failure visibility. 
PagerDuty grew on outages. GitHub Actions shows failures. AWS shows alarms.
Companies absolutely monetize failure visibility.

New: Need product-specific, capability-based arguments, not just incentive-based.
The real question isn't "Why won't they build it?" — it's "What stops them 
from acquiring you?"
