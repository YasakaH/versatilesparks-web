EXECUTION INTEGRITY — COURSE CORRECTION
Date: 2026-07-19

# DIAGNOSIS

The journey lost the original abstraction:

  Data integrity
    → Execution integrity
    → Automation reliability
    → AI agent reliability
    → AI agent debugger (too small)

Agent Replay may be a good developer tool, but it is not the
original company thesis. It risks becoming:
  AI debugging tool → AI observability → competes with LangSmith/Langfuse

That is the crowded AI tooling ecosystem we set out to avoid.

# THE CORRECTION

The invariant was never "AI." It was:

  "Truth preservation in automated systems."

The four primitives were the actual discovery:
  1. Identity — who performed the action?
  2. Intent — was this the intended action?
  3. Evidence — what proves it happened?
  4. Time — when did it happen?

Those are not AI concepts. They are DATA INTEGRITY PRIMITIVES.

# THE REVISED COMPANY THESIS

"As software becomes autonomous, organizations lose visibility into
why decisions happen and whether actions are trustworthy. We build an
integrity layer that records, verifies, and proves automated execution
across AI agents, workflows, and enterprise systems."

Not "Agent Replay" — but "Execution Ledger" or "Trust Layer for Automation."

# THE PRODUCT HIERARCHY (Corrected)

  Execution Ledger (tamper-evident record of automated actions)
    → Replay becomes a feature
    → Debugging becomes a feature
    → Compliance becomes a feature
    → Governance becomes a feature

# THE WEDGE QUESTION (Unanswered)

What is the smallest product that creates a trusted execution record,
without being trapped as an AI debugging tool?

Candidates (where integrity failure has immediate financial cost):
  1. Financial automation integrity (payments, refunds, invoices)
  2. Enterprise automation integrity (CRM, ERP, HR systems)
  3. AI agent governance (verify authorization before action)

# THE DECISION

Optimize for "strongest unavoidable future need," not "easiest adoption."
