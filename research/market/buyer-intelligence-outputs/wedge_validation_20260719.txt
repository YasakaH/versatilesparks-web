# The central question

"What is the first automated action where a company cannot afford to
say: 'We don't know exactly what happened'?"

# Wedge candidates evaluated

## Candidate 1: Financial Automation Integrity

Who writes the check?
  CFO / VP of Finance / Head of Risk & Controls
  Budget line: operational risk, internal controls, fraud prevention

What incident causes urgency?
  An automated refund or payment goes wrong. Money leaves the company.
  Auditor asks: "Show me the evidence that this payment was authorized."
  Answer: "We don't know" → immediate escalation.

What are they paying today?
  Manual audit ($200-500/hr per auditor), chargeback losses, fraud losses
  Reconciliation teams (3-5 FTE at $80K+ each)
  Insurance deductibles for payment errors

What system do we sit beside?
  Payment gateways (Stripe, Adyen), ERP (SAP, Oracle), billing systems
  We verify AFTER the payment API call but BEFORE funds leave

Can we deploy without replacing anything?
  Yes. API wrapper layer. Instrument the payment call, not replace it.

Can we prove ROI in 30 days?
  Yes. Catch one unauthorized refund → save the full amount → immediate ROI.

Trigger event:
  "A payment was made and we can't prove who authorized it."
  Frequency: daily in large organizations.

## Candidate 2: Enterprise Automation Integrity

Who writes the check?
  CIO / Head of Automation / CISO
  Budget: automation governance, internal controls

What incident causes urgency?
  An RPA bot or enterprise workflow modifies critical data without trace.
  Compliance audit finds undocumented system changes.

What are they paying today?
  Compliance penalties, audit remediation, manual reconciliation
  Failed SOC2 / SOX controls

What system do we sit beside?
  RPA platforms (UiPath, AA), iPaaS (Workato, MuleSoft), ERP (SAP, Oracle)
  We capture execution evidence at the API/event level

Can we deploy without replacing anything?
  Yes. Event listener pattern. Instrument the automation platform output.

Can we prove ROI in 30 days?
  Harder. Value is risk-avoidance, not direct cost savings. Longer sales cycle.

Trigger event:
  "Our auditor couldn't verify who changed this record."
  Frequency: quarterly (audit-driven).

## Candidate 3: AI Agent Governance

Who writes the check?
  Head of AI / CTO / Chief AI Ethics Officer
  Budget: AI governance, responsible AI

What incident causes urgency?
  An AI agent takes an unauthorized action (refunds $50K, deletes records).
  Board or regulator demands: "What controls are in place?"

What are they paying today?
  AI governance consultants, manual review of agent actions
  Insurance premiums for AI liability

What system do we sit beside?
  AI agent frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen), LLM APIs
  We wrap the tool execution layer

Can we deploy without replacing anything?
  Yes. Decorator / middleware pattern. Framework-agnostic.

Can we prove ROI in 30 days?
  Strong. Catch one bad agent action → immediate cost avoidance.

Trigger event:
  "Our AI agent did something we didn't authorize."
  Frequency: growing weekly. Early market.

## Candidate 4: Automation Change Protection (Execution Gate)

Who writes the check?
  CIO / Head of Automation
  Budget: IT controls, change management

What incident causes urgency?
  An automated workflow changed behavior between runs.
  "Yesterday it worked. Today it didn't. Nobody changed the code."

What are they paying today?
  Incident response, production outages, manual rollback
  Fragile automation breaks weekly — 45% RPA failure rate

What system do we sit beside?
  Any automation (scripts, bots, AI agents, RPA, CI/CD pipelines)
  We capture before/after state and detect change

Can we deploy without replacing anything?
  Yes. Instrumentation layer around any executable.

Can we prove ROI in 30 days?
  Yes. Detect one behavioral drift → prevent one outage → immediate savings.

Trigger event:
  "The automation ran successfully but produced the wrong result."
  Frequency: weekly in any org with >10 automated workflows.

# Ranking by startup probability

Rank criteria: Pain × Timing × Accessibility × Founder Advantage

1. Financial Automation Integrity
   Pain: immediate financial loss
   Timing: AI agents increasingly touch payment systems
   Accessibility: API wrapper, measurable ROI, budget exists today
   Founder advantage: strong (case database + four primitives)

2. AI Agent Governance
   Pain: growing, visible in headlines
   Timing: perfect (AI agent adoption accelerating)
   Accessibility: developer-led, can start open-source
   Founder advantage: strong (built Agent Replay, understands space)
   Risk: crowded market forming

3. Enterprise Automation Integrity
   Pain: real but diffuse
   Timing: early (enterprise not yet feeling pain acutely)
   Accessibility: long enterprise sales cycle
   Founder advantage: moderate

4. Automation Change Protection
   Pain: real, large (45% RPA breakage rate)
   Timing: steady
   Accessibility: technical sale, less crowded
   Founder advantage: moderate
