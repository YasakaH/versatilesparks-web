# THE CORRECTED PRODUCT THESIS

Not: Record → Replay → Expire
But: **Expire** (TTL-bound risk freshness)

Insurance isn't about history. It's about CURRENT RISK.
Risk changes as agents drift. Static attestations are worthless.

The TTL concept is more differentiated than recording itself.

# THE MARKETING FRAMING

Not: AI Replay, Execution Ledger, AI Notary, Trace Recorder
But: **Independent AI Evidence Infrastructure**

Because multiple parties need to trust the same evidence:
  enterprise + insurer + auditor + regulator + court

Multi-party trust is where independent infrastructure wins over
vendor-specific tooling.
