## This Is a Proposal — Not a Finished Standard

This is Version 0.1 of a living proposal. It has been internally stress-tested against five independent knowledge domains (Perception, Decision Engine, Planning, Scheduling, Execution), but it has not yet been evaluated by engineers outside the project.

You are being asked to help answer three questions before wider publication.

---

## Three Questions

**1. What idea was most memorable?**

If nothing stuck with you after reading, the manifesto failed its own Whiteboard test. Tell me what you remember — or tell me that you remember nothing. Both are useful.

**2. Where did you stop believing the argument?**

This is the most important question. If you stopped believing at page 2, say so. It tells me exactly where the evidence is weak, where the framing is overreaching, or where the logic breaks down. Don't hold back — I'd rather hear that than polite disagreement after publication.

**3. What important concept do you think is missing?**

Every framework has blind spots. Tell me yours.

---

## Optional Fourth Question (Engineers Only)

**4. Would this framework change how you design a production agent system? Why or why not?**

Not "do you agree?" but "would it actually change your work?" If you'd walk away from this and design systems the same way, that's valuable information about whether the framework has practical leverage beyond academic framing.
