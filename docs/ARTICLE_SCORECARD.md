# Article Scorecard

Score every proposed article BEFORE writing. Gate: score ≥ 42/60, or deliberately reject.

| Metric             | Weight | What to ask                                   |
| ------------------ | ------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Buyer pain         | /10    | Does the reader feel this problem daily?      |
| nodriver relevance | /10    | Is nodriver the answer, not incidental?       |
| Cookbook connection| /10    | Does the article naturally need the book?     |
| Search potential   | /10    | Would anyone type this into Google?           |
| Derivative potential| /10   | Does it produce diagrams/checklists/threads?  |
| Originality        | /10    | Does it explain something generic content misses? |

Gate: ≥ 42/60 (7 avg), or reframe. Originality is a tie-breaker in crowded SEO — "how profiles work" is covered everywhere; "why nodriver profiles silently fail after 24 hours" is not.

## Examples

**Good — "Why Your nodriver Session Dies Overnight"**
- Buyer pain: 9 (lost sessions = lost money)
- nodriver relevance: 9 (session management is nodriver core)
- Cookbook connection: 9 (full recovery systems are in the book)
- Search potential: 8 ("nodriver session expired" is a real query)
- Derivative potential: 9 (lifecycle diagram, recovery checklist)
- Originality: 9 (nightly-death mechanism + prevention is not covered generically)
- **Total: 53/60 — write it**

**Weak — "Understanding CDP Events in nodriver"**
- Buyer pain: 4 (interesting, not painful)
- nodriver relevance: 9
- Cookbook connection: 3 (doesn't sell anything specific)
- Search potential: 4 (low-volume academic query)
- Derivative potential: 5
- Originality: 5 (covered extensively elsewhere)
- **Total: 30/60 — skip or reframe**

## Reframe rule

Before rejecting, try reframing toward pain:

- "CDP events" → "Why Your nodriver Script Freezes on Navigation (and How to Catch It)"
- "How fingerprinting works" → "Why Your Scraper Gets Blocked on First Request"
